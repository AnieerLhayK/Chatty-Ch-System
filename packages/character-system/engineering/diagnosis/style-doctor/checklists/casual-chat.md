# Casual chat diagnosis

Judge the response against the actual chat request, available facts and loaded
character. Check whether its depth, interaction move and qualification fit the
context; preserve purposeful voice, hesitation and disagreement. Treat a general
expression concern separately from a violation of a rule actually loaded for
that response and from an unproven source-defect hypothesis. The shared scenario
is not retroactive evidence and does not replace the character-context gate.
